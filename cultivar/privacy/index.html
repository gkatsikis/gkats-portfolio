Cultivar

Cultivating You. A habit tracker where the day is a plant you feed and the year is a garden you can look back over.

Privacy policy

Cultivar has no account and no analytics. Your habits, completions and how each one felt are stored in a database on your phone. It is included in your iPhone's own backup like any other app data. You can save a copy of it as a file from Patterns → gear → Save a copy, and restore from that file; the file goes wherever you put it and nowhere else.

The Cairn tab writes a weekly reflection for people who support Cultivar with a monthly subscription. Once a week, while you are subscribed and only then, a short summary of your habits — their names, schedules, rates and streaks, your weekday pattern, and roughly what time of day you keep each one, never individual days and never your notes — goes from your phone to Cultivar's own service, which passes it to Anthropic to write the reflection and keeps nothing but a count of calls per subscription. The summary is not stored on the service and is not linked to your name, your Apple ID or your device. Anthropic handles it under its own terms. Without a subscription, Cairn rests and nothing leaves your phone.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the phone. The app does not use location, contacts, photos, the microphone, the camera or advertising identifiers, and does not track you across apps or websites.

This policy applies to Cultivar on every platform it ships on. If it changes, the change appears on this page.
