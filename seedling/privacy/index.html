Seedling

A meditation timer that counts up and asks for nothing, a meditation teacher's writings and guided meditations, and friends to sit with.

Privacy policy

Without an account

Seedling works without an account, and without one nothing about you leaves your phone. Your sits, your settings, the writings you keep and the guided meditations you have heard are stored in a database on your phone. You can save a copy of it as a file from Settings → Save a copy, and restore from that file; the file goes wherever you put it and nowhere else.

The app downloads writings and recordings from its own site. Nothing about you is sent with those requests.

If Android backup is turned on, the database is included in your phone's backup to your Google account. Downloaded recordings and pages are left out; they come back from the site.

Seedling has no analytics and no ads, does not use an advertising identifier, location, contacts, the microphone or the camera, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Together

Together is optional. If you sign in to it, Seedling's server keeps:

  • your name, your three-word code, and a photo if you choose one. Your friends see them; the members of a circle you are in see your code;
  • your latest sit: its date, when it started and how long it was, which your friends see, and whether you are sitting right now;
  • who your friends are, the times you agree to sit together, and the time you have sat together;
  • a copy of your phone's database, as above, so that a new phone gets it back when you sign in. Nobody sees it but you, through the app;
  • a code that lets your phone receive invitations (a push token).

These stay until you delete your account. Signing out removes the push token and leaves the rest in place for your next sign-in.

To stop sign-in codes being requested by the thousand, the server counts requests from each internet address for a minute, and keeps nothing of them afterwards.

Signing in

By email: the address is sent to Brevo, which delivers your sign-in code. The server keeps a one-way fingerprint of the address, so that the next sign-in finds the same account, and never the address itself.

With Google: Google tells the server which Google account you are. The server keeps that account's id and the same fingerprint of its address.

Who handles your data

Nobody buys or is given your data. These services handle it for Seedling and for nothing else: Cloudflare runs the server and its database, Brevo sends the sign-in emails, and Google delivers invitations to your phone (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and, if you use it, the Google sign-in. Everything travels encrypted.

Notifications

The only notifications come from Together: a friend asking you to sit, a friend joining you, and the time you agreed to sit. The app sends none of its own, apart from the notice Android requires while a sit or a recording is running.

Deleting your account

In the app: Together, your photo or initial at the top right, then Delete account. Or on the web, at seedling-together.gkats.workers.dev/delete. Everything the server holds about you is deleted at once: your name, photo and code, your friendships and circles, the times you agreed, your latest sit and the copy of your phone. What is on your phone stays until you remove the app.
