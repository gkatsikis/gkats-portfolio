Seedling

A meditation timer for Android that counts up and asks for nothing. Press start, sit, press end. It keeps one number: the time you have sat, all of it.

Beside the timer, a meditation teacher's own writings, in the teacher's hand and read aloud, and guided meditations you can keep on the phone for when there is no signal.

Together is for friends: they see when you last sat, and can ask you to sit with them at a time. That is the only notification the app sends.

Made for e-ink readers first, and at home on any phone.
